#### For smallholder farmers / upstream

Indonesian: Menyediakan mekanisme pendanaan perdagangan (trade finance) yang transparan dan aman berbasis inventaris barang fisik yang tersimpan di gudang

English: Delivering transparent, secure trade finance mechanisms backed by physical inventory held in verified warehouses
